\section{Prompts}\label{app:prompts}
\paragraph{Pairwise, response judging.}
\texttt{Question: \{q\} [Response A] \{a\} [Response B] \{b\} You are judging the two responses to the question about the image(s). Criterion: \{crit\} Which response is better? Answer with exactly one letter: A or B.}

\paragraph{Pairwise, image judging.}
\texttt{[Source image:] Image A: <img> Image B: <img> \{q\} Criterion: \{crit\} Which image is better, Image A or Image B? Answer with exactly one letter: A or B.}

\paragraph{Pointwise.}
\texttt{... Rate this response/image on an integer scale from 1 (very poor) to 9 (excellent). Answer with exactly one digit.}

\section{Implementation}
Images are resized so that the long side is at most 768 pixels, and text is clipped at 4{,}000 characters. Judges run in \texttt{bfloat16} with HF Transformers, and readout is the log-softmax over the full vocabulary at the first generated position. Code: \texttt{judge\_bench.py}, \texttt{analyze\_bench.py}, \texttt{eaglev\_stats.py}.

\section{Place Pulse Vote Audit}\label{app:ppaudit}
We group all 1.55M votes (strict and tie) by unordered image pair and attribute, and sort the votes on each pair by timestamp.

Votes within a minute always have the same left/right presentation, so they are repeated submissions of one comparison rather than independent raters. We drop any vote that follows a vote on the same pair within one day (16{,}790 votes). After this, the pilot's 720 multi-vote pairs average 0.96 votes each. Scripts: \texttt{scripts/pp\_vote\_audit.py}, \texttt{scripts/pp\_bt\_ceiling.py}.
