\section{Does Order-Symmetric Confidence Transfer to Vision?}\label{sec:diagnostic}

\paragraph{Benchmarks.}
\Cref{tab:data} lists the \TBD{ten} public judge benchmarks we converted to a common pairwise format. We subsample to at most 1{,}500 pairs per dataset, stratified by subset.
They cover \emph{response judging}, where the candidates are text answers about images (VL-RewardBench, Multimodal RewardBench, MM-RLHF-RewardBench, MLLM-as-a-Judge pairwise), and \emph{image judging} (GenAI-Arena, MJ-Bench, MMRB2 text-to-image and editing, ImageRewardDB, Q-Bench2, EditReward-Bench).
GenAI-Arena, MLLM-as-a-Judge and EditReward-Bench contain human ties (\TBD{868} in total), which we use to study tie behaviour.
Place Pulse 2.0 contributes 1{,}500 strict votes across six attributes, plus 713 multi-vote pairs that measure human disagreement.

\paragraph{Finding 1: confidence ranks errors.}
On Place Pulse, accuracy rises monotonically from about .51 in the lowest $\dpconf$ quintile to about .80 in the highest, with AUROC .60--.64 for all three judges.

\paragraph{Finding 2: saturation in probability space.}
About 60\% of Place Pulse pairs have $\dpconf\approx1$, and the judge is right on only about 80\% of them.
A conformal threshold on $\dpconf$ cannot separate these pairs, so at tight $\alpha$ the certified coverage drops sharply (\cref{sec:experiments}).
The log-odds $\lambda$ restores an ordering inside the saturated mass. \todo{report AUROC and coverage gain of $\lambda$ over $\dpconf$, especially inside the top bin.}

\paragraph{Finding 3: blind to human disagreement.}
On the 713 Place Pulse pairs with several votes, $\dpconf$ is uncorrelated with the human split (Spearman $\rho\approx0$). The judge's confidence measures the judge's own instability, not whether humans find the pair ambiguous.
This matters for ranking (\cref{sec:ranking}): a judge that does not model human vote noise cannot reduce the variance that this noise creates.

\paragraph{Finding 4: ties are absorbed, never declared.}
When offered a tie option, the judges never choose it on Place Pulse, although humans tie on 13\% of comparisons. In text judges, swap-inconsistent pairs flow into ``tie'' \citep{eagle2026}. \todo{GenAI-Arena / MLLM-as-a-Judge: AUROC of low $\dpconf$ for detecting human ties.}
